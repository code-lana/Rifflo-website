Quick Guides & Docs

User Documentation & Best Practices

Get the most out of your experience with these quick walkthrough guides.

01

Creating Your Account

Launch Rifflo and enter your email address to register. Your session is securely authenticated through Supabase GoTrue, ensuring immediate cloud backup of your companions.

02

Setting Your Mood

Before starting a chat, use the Mood Bar to indicate your current state (e.g. Feeling Low, Stressed, Excited). Rifflo adapts its empathy level and conversational cadence accordingly.

03

Personalizing Companions

Head over to Profile > Companions to rename your companions (e.g. Mom, Bestie, Grandma), customize what they call you, and upload custom photos.

04

Privacy & Permanent Purge

Under Settings, you have one-click access to "Wipe All Data". This triggers a GDPR/CCPA compliant purge that irreversibly erases your chats and account from the database.

Privacy Policy & Safety Commitment

🛡️ How We Protect Your Privacy

  • Row Level Security (RLS): Enforced at PostgreSQL database level. No other user can ever access or query your chat history.
  • Zero Third-Party Ad Trackers: We do not integrate tracking SDKs like Google AdMob or Facebook Pixel.
  • Encrypted Communications: All requests travel through HTTPS and TLS 1.3 encrypted tunnels directly to Supabase Edge Functions.
